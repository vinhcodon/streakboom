# Code of Conduct

Streakboom is a small, gentle project, and its space should feel the same. This code is short and is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (version 2.1, CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part, whatever their experience, background, identity, language or ability, without harassment. (The app itself is made for adults, 18 and over.)

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to English.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. Assume good faith.
- Give and take feedback gracefully; say "I do not understand" when you do not.
- Keep to the topic of the project in issues and pull requests.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual attention, threats, publishing someone else's private information, spam, and anything else that would reasonably make a person feel unwelcome.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every space of the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, and when someone represents the project in public). The maintainer, Vinh (`@vinhcodon`), may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code. To report a problem, use the private reporting option on the repository's Security tab (Report a vulnerability), or open an issue asking for a private channel **without** details. Reports are handled with care and kept private.
