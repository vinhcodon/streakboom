# ADR-0002: Mass market first; a naive pet first, a brained pet second

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** alpha 1.0 planning (original requirements, undated)
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), with Claude

## Context
Power users are a small wave; most people will not paste an API key before they care about the pet.

## Decision
The audience is the mass market first, developers later. The pet is lovable with no AI (naive stage); a key is requested later, from a behavioral signal, never at install, always dismissible (stage two).

## Alternatives considered
A chat-first product that needs a key on day one (rejected).

## Consequences and when to revisit
Most users may stay naive, so skin packs may carry more of the value (monetization is its own hypothesis).
