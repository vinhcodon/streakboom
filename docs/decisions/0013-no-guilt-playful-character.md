# ADR-0013: The pet never dies; the tone is playful and mildly sarcastic, with a gentle switch

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** alpha 1.0 planning (original requirements, undated)
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), with Claude

## Context
A companion should not punish absence; sarcasm lands badly with some people and moods.

## Decision
The pet never dies, starves or gets sick; neglect only makes it a bit lonely. Teasing targets the situation, never the user's worth or work. A Gentle tone setting drops the sarcastic lines and the sassy reaction.

## Alternatives considered
Tamagotchi-style decay (rejected).

## Consequences and when to revisit
Line pools need hand-written, reviewed content in two languages (182 per language today).
