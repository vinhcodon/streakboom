# ADR-0050: Sticker second pass: some sarcasm, a lounging cat, a curled-up sleeper

- **Status:** accepted (answered by the author on 2026-11-11); drawn as a first pass, not yet looked at by the author
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), with Claude
- **Supersedes in part:** ADR-0049 item 3 (no sarcasm in the first set), item 6 (sticker 3 as a rolled-over cat, "Gãi bụng đi?") and the pose of sticker 9

## Context
The author found sticker 3 (belly) and 9 (good night, side-on loaf) unnatural, asked for new poses and expressions, and for a share of sarcasm, based on what a sticker is used for. The reasoning (a sticker is a person talking to a person, banter is its main use, the set was too gentle) is in `docs/plans/sticker-set-v1-direction.md`.

## Decision
1. **Sarcasm share about 29%: 5 of 17 stickers** tease: 03, 05 "Hừm.", 15 "Hì hì.", and two new ones, 16 "Ừ, giỏi lắm." and 17 "Ai đợi đâu." The sarcasm is a deadpan face against a mild line (half lid, gaze aside, strong cheeks for "secretly glad"); it teases the situation, never the person's worth, and stays inside Zalo's rules (no insults).
2. **Sticker 03** becomes a cat lying back at ease with one leg crossed over the other, head propped, "thinking about life" (the author's own idea), with the text **"Đời là thế."** (3 words, the author's choice, a deadpan shrug). Sticker-only art (the app cat does not lie like this).
3. **Sticker 09 "Ngủ ngon."** becomes a cat curled into a round loaf like a mochi, head on its paws, tail wrapped round the front. Sticker-only art (the app has the loaf, not the curl).
4. Not taken: the belly-up dare "Gãi đi, dám không?", the sweet sit-up belly, the front loaf for sleep, and the extra sarcastic ones "Rồi sao nữa?", "Đang bận ngủ.", "Biết ngay.", "Hứ." (kept in the direction document as ideas for a later pack).

## Later the same day
The author found the cat lying back with crossed legs not relaxed enough, three softer poses were drawn (`docs/plans/assets/stickers/direction-v1/03_options.png`), and he judged all three harder to read than the first, so **the crossed-leg version stays** as sticker 03 ("Đời là thế."). The three options are kept only as a record.

## Later the same day (2)
After looking at the set the author asked for: 04 "Buồn ngủ…" to look sleepy (now closed eyes, a small yawn, a tipped head and a "z"); 10 "Trời mưa rồi." with the cloud no longer under the text; 07 "Giỏi quá!" with the paws clapping; and 16 "Ừ, giỏi lắm." changed to **"Giỏi quá."** with a thumbs-up. 16 keeps its half-lidded, flat-mouthed face, so it still teases (a deadpan thumbs-up); if that does not read as dry at phone size, the sarcasm share is 4 of 17 (about 24%). 07 and 16 now carry nearly the same words ("Giỏi quá!" with a clap, "Giỏi quá." with a thumbs-up): a point to look at again.

## Later the same day (3)
The clapping 07 "Giỏi quá!" was removed (it duplicated 16, the thumbs-up "Giỏi quá."). The set is 16 stickers; the sarcasm share is 5 of 16 (about 31%: 03, 05, 15 "Hì hì.", 16 "Giỏi quá.", 17 "Ai đợi đâu."). The expression audit is `docs/plans/sticker-expression-audit.md`.

## Consequences
- Spec 06 section 7 and the set list are updated: 16 are in the app (matched to a state) and the sticker-only art is now 03, 09 and the cloud of 10; the set is 17 stickers.
- The sarcasm texts have not been through the author's ear for Vietnamese banter (backlog E3).
- Revisit when the set is looked at on a phone (V-94) and after the run (plan section 5).
