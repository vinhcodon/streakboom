# ADR-0054: The website follows the app in 0.3.2: the fifth promise, climbing, the retired settings, the new senses

- **Status:** accepted (answered by the author on 2026-11-11); **built on 2026-11-11** (committed; the live behaviour is checked after the push)
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), with Claude (the website session), after the release prompt for 0.3.2 (ADR-0033)

## Context
Version 0.3.2 made Peak sit and climb on window edges, fall to the window below, hop out from under the Start menu, be teased at an edge, start walks and lines only after about 45 seconds without input, and it **removed the settings Chattiness and Quiet hours**. It also made the coarse picture of where windows are **on by default** (a 16 by 9 grid, an anonymous number for which squares are the same window, memory only, switch in Settings, Privacy), added a "covered or not" check for the Start menu, and a short remark that a connected brain may add after a fall (only the event name and five numbers are sent).

The site review found what is no longer true or is missing: the "Polite" card, the "alive without AI" card and the hero caption of the sleeping scene still say Peak is quiet "during quiet hours" (three languages; the setting is gone); the "What Peak notices" text on the privacy page and the How it works page says Peak reads only a few things and says nothing of the window picture or the covered check; the privacy page says nothing of the short remark; "falls to the bottom of the screen" is now "to the window or ledge below"; the climbing is not on the site at all. Twelve strings (`p1` to `p7`, `priv_title`, `glance_title`, `not_title`, `nav_updates`, `latest_release`) are no longer used by any page; they are copies that can go stale (`p7` repeated the sensor text).

## Decision
1. **The retired settings.** The "Polite" card says: quiet in fullscreen apps, **at night (23:00 to 07:00 by the clock)** and in Quiet mode; Peak decides when to speak and speaks on its own only when you are not working. The hero caption of the sleeping scene and the first feature card lose "quiet hours" in English, Vietnamese and Japanese. The word list that catches outdated wording gets "quiet hours" and "chattiness" (and the Vietnamese and Japanese forms), so they cannot come back.
2. **The privacy text follows the README** in three languages, on the privacy page and the How it works page: the **coarse picture of where windows are** (a grid of squares, the bare desktop, the taskbar or some window, an anonymous number for the same window; on by default, kept in memory only, never saved or sent, no titles, names or contents; switched off in Settings, Privacy), used to find the top edge of a window to sit on and the side to climb; the **covered or not** check when the Start menu or Search opens over Peak; and the **short remark after a fall** (a connected brain may write one sentence; only the name of the event and five numbers about how it feels are sent; it counts toward the daily limit; Settings, Brain). It still says Peak never reads titles, contents, app names or keys, and never says Peak "sees" the screen. The wording "under its feet" stays (the build checks it).
3. **The fifth promise, wording A** (chosen by the author): "While you work it keeps still and quiet. It wanders or speaks up on its own only when you have stopped for a while." It is placed second (the order of the charter and the README), so the block becomes "Five things you can count on". It leans on the README sentence "stays still and quiet" (the build checks that it is there). It does not promise that Peak never moves: that it makes way, hops out from under the Start menu and falls when its window goes is said in the feature cards. The promise is added to the home page, the top of the privacy page, `llms-full.txt` and the build's list of promises.
4. **Climbing: a tenth feature card, "Climbs on your windows"** on the How it works page (about 70 words): it hops up to the top edge of a window or walks to its side and climbs it, climbs down again, never walks off a ledge (it stops, looks over the edge, then turns back, sits or climbs down); it falls to the window or ledge below when its window goes away; poke it near the end of a ledge and it wobbles (four times in five it catches itself, otherwise it falls beside the wall and keeps away from edges for ten minutes); let go of it above the floor and it falls. The card "Out of your way" says it hops out from under the Start menu and Search and falls to the window below. The home page keeps four feature lines; the "Out of your way" line gains a clause about climbing. The number of feature cards is counted from the strings (ten), not written in the code: `featureList` of the structured data, the cards of the How it works page, `llms-full.txt` and the build checks follow it.
5. **The hero tour gets no new scene** (the author's choice): as for the fall, the page has no window to climb; the behaviour is told in the cards.
6. **One new question on the How it works page:** "Does Peak get in the way when I work?" with a one or two sentence answer (no: it keeps still and quiet while you work and wanders or speaks on its own only after you have stopped for about 45 seconds; it is quiet in fullscreen apps, at night and in Quiet mode). It is in the structured data of that page and in `llms-full.txt`.
7. **The unused strings are removed** (the fifteen above in three languages), so that no hidden copy of the privacy text can go stale; a build check fails on a string key that nothing uses.

8. **An image sitemap** (the author's choice): each sticker page lists its 20 pictures in `sitemap.xml` (`image:loc` only: search engines no longer use the title or caption tags). The build checks that each page's list is the manifest's.
9. **A social picture for the sticker pages** (the author's choice): the collage `preview_440.png` made by the sticker session (440 by 440, Vietnamese text, nine stickers), kept as the site asset `site/assets/sticker-preview.png` (a copy, because `set-v0`, where it is generated, is to be retired), with a `summary` card; the other pages keep the site picture. It is a small square and has Vietnamese text on the English and Japanese pages; how Zalo, Telegram and Messenger show it has not been seen.
10. **The How it works page tells "what Peak notices" in two short paragraphs** and links to the privacy page, which alone holds the exact text (the window picture, the covered check, the short remark): a fact in one place, so the next change of the senses touches one page, not two.
11. **The fixed question set is asked again on 2026-12-01** (about three weeks after Search Console and Bing were set up) and compared with the baseline of 2026-11-10 (plan section 12).

## Result (2026-11-11)
Built as above. The build now also fails on: the words "quiet hours" and "chattiness" (and their Vietnamese and Japanese forms) anywhere but in the release notes; a string key that nothing uses; a promise that is missing or has no README sentence behind it (the fifth leans on "stays still and quiet", which the README has, so the app session need not change it, only keep it); an image sitemap or a share picture that does not match the sticker set. The number of feature cards (ten) is counted from the strings. The home page shows the newest update's first **sentence** (the 0.3.2 line is long). Measured (English words; VI and JA in the plan): home 711, How it works 1,493 (it gained a card, a question and the climbing text, and lost the long senses text), privacy 891 (it gained the new senses and the remark), `llms-full.txt` 3,048 (the promises, the stickers and the card were added). The budgets of ADR-0048 are still exceeded for the last three. 90 tests in `scripts/site/`.

## Alternatives considered
The fifth promise in the shorter form (only "quiet", which could be read as "it never moves") or with the exception inside the promise (longer, a heavier block); climbing folded into the "Out of your way" card (about 110 words, buried), or not described; a tour scene of a climb, of a look over an edge, or both (the tour would grow from 46 to 51 or 56 seconds, and the page cannot show a window); no new question on the How it works page.

## Consequences
- The privacy text is longer again in three languages; the Vietnamese and Japanese have no native reader.
- The promise block becomes a bigger commitment: "keeps still and quiet while you work" is checked against the README sentence only, not against the app (that is the app session's audit, epic E15 and its tests).
- Not verified when this record was written: everything above is a decision; whether the 0.3.2 behaviour is as the README and the CHANGELOG say (only read, not run); the state on the Pages host.
