# ADR-0057: The mission is "a small friend that makes the workday lighter"; no healing or health claims

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), with Claude

## Context
The first vision said Peak should heal people psychologically. The research found almost no evidence that an on-screen pet helps; evidence for real pets is weak and inconsistent. FDA General Wellness guidance and the EU MDR treat advertising and sales words as evidence of intended purpose.

## Decision
The mission is: *a small friend who makes the workday lighter and leads you back to real life.* Success means people live better when they use Peak less, not more. The app, README, website, store listings and stickers do not say "healing", "therapy", "clinically proven", or that Peak reduces anxiety, depression, ADHD or loneliness. The do/don't-say table in the foundation is the check.

## Alternatives considered
Keeping "healing" as the mission (rejected: no evidence, regulatory risk). Dropping the mission (rejected: the corner-of-the-screen friend is the product).

## Consequences and when to revisit
Every public text needs one audit against the table before 0.4.0. Revisit if Streakboom runs its own measured study and the results support a narrower, evidence-based claim, and then only with legal review.
