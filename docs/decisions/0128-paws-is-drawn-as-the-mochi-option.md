# ADR-0128: Paws is drawn as option A, "Mochi"

- **Status:** accepted on 2026-11-15 (the author's answer)
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Decided by:** Vinh (author), recorded by the sticker session

## Context
The first reference sheet of Paws (ADR-0127) was judged not as cute as Peak. Three cuter options were drawn (`docs/plans/paws-options.md`): A Mochi, B Big head, C Sitting.

## Decision
**Paws is option A, "Mochi":** head and body are one round bean (like Peak), soft floppy ears, white blaze and belly, white toed paws, tan brow spots, a nub tail with a white tip, the light rim line. Files: `docs/plans/assets/paws/paws-option-a.svg`.

## Consequences
- The floppy ears move Paws away from the classic upright Border Collie look; the author accepted this for cuteness.
- The first sheet (`paws-reference-sheet.*`) is superseded as the look of Paws; its palette and rim rules stay.
- A side view and the eight stickers of spec 19 follow; not drawn yet.
