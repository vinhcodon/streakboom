# Licenses of the dependencies

**Checked on 2026-11-12** (story CP-3, one of the conditions of SignPath Foundation: an OSI-approved license for every component and no proprietary component; [`plans/signpath-ci-plan.md`](plans/signpath-ci-plan.md)). Streakboom itself is under the [MIT license](../LICENSE). Nothing proprietary was found.

## What was checked, and how

- **Rust** (the program that is compiled into `streakboom.exe`): `cargo metadata --locked --format-version 1 --filter-platform x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` in `app/src-tauri/`, reading the `license` field of each of the **303** packages in the Windows build graph (without our own crate).
- **JavaScript:** the production dependency of the app is one package, `@tauri-apps/api` (MIT or Apache-2.0). The build tools (SvelteKit, Svelte, Vite, svelte-check and their dependencies, **61** installed packages in all) are used at build time and their runtime parts (Svelte's small runtime) are compiled into the front end; a scan of `node_modules/*/package.json` found MIT (50), Apache-2.0 (4), Apache-2.0 OR MIT (3), MPL-2.0 (2), ISC (1) and BSD-3-Clause (1), and no GPL, AGPL, LGPL, unlicensed or commercial license.

## The result

| License (as written in the package) | Packages | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MIT, Apache-2.0, "MIT OR Apache-2.0" and their combinations | about 250 | The usual Rust dual license |
| Unicode-3.0 | 18 (the `icu_*` family and its helpers) | OSI-approved; the Unicode data license |
| Unlicense OR MIT, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, Zlib, 0BSD, CC0-1.0 | about 25 | Permissive |
| **MPL-2.0** | 5 (`cssparser`, `cssparser-macros`, `dtoa-short`, `option-ext`, `selectors`) | OSI-approved, file-level copyleft: a modified MPL file must be shared under the MPL. We use these crates **unmodified**, from crates.io |
| **CDLA-Permissive-2.0** | 2 (`webpki-roots`, two versions: Mozilla's list of trusted certificate authorities, as data) | A permissive license for **data**; it is **not on the OSI list of software licenses**. It covers the certificate list compiled into the update and chat connections. To be named in the SignPath application and, if a reviewer objects, replaced by the system's certificate store (the `rustls-native-certs` route) |

## What is not done

- **No notice of third-party licenses is shipped with the installer.** MIT, Apache-2.0 and BSD ask that their copyright notices travel with a binary. This is not a SignPath condition, but it is a duty: story **CP-4** (generate a `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES` file in the build and put it in the installer and the repository).
- **The art, the lines and the persona** are ours (the art is generated by our code); the Japanese lines were written by Claude, and the stickers are under CC BY 4.0 (a separate license, see ADR-0051).
- **The licenses were read from the packages' own `license` fields,** not from their source files; a package that declares the wrong license would not be caught.

## How to repeat it

```powershell
cd app\src-tauri
cargo metadata --locked --format-version 1 --filter-platform x86_64-pc-windows-msvc > meta.json   # then count the "license" fields of the packages in "resolve"
cd ..
npm ls --omit=dev --all
```

Do it again before an application to SignPath and whenever a dependency is added (the new dependency's license is part of the change, like its size).
