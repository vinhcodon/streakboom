'
Written 2026-11-22 by Claude Code (the app session) for a reader who did not take part: Codex or another reviewer, and the author. **Read, do not change.** Do not commit to this repository, do not write an ADR number (the app session gives them), do not run the program with a real key.'

- **A branch**, `exp/duo-prototype` (31 commits ahead of `main`, 51 files changed in `app/` and `scripts/`). It is a **Preview-only experiment behind the developer flag `STREAKBOOM_DUO=1`, never to be merged without the author**; the released program does not change except one small shared fix (below). Start from [`../plans/duo-prototype-notes.md`](../plans/duo-prototype-notes.md): what was built, how to run it, what was checked, what was seen, what is not verified.'

The design behind 0141 is [`../plans/duo-conversation-design.md`](../plans/duo-conversation-design.md).'
- **The open items**, one by one, in [`../plans/verification-debt.md`](../plans/verification-debt.md), V-179 to V-187.'

## How to run what can be run'
From `app/`: `npm install`, `npm test` (491 tests), `npm run check`, then `cd src-tauri; cargo test` (135). From the repo root: `node scripts/audit-docs.mjs`. A build of the prototype and its flags are in the notes (section "How to run and check it").'
'
## The three questions for every prototype (section 6 of `duo-prototype-and-cross-review.md`)'
1. **Does it break any article of the charter?** Articles 2 (the attention is theirs: the box never opens by itself, a pet never writes into it unprompted, no unread count, no read receipt, the speech of two pets is not more than the speech of one), 5 (a companion, not an assistant: Paws is told it is not one, the box has no copy, regenerate or attach), 7 (no pet needs the person) are the ones at risk.'
2. **Is there a cost of attention or resources not measured?** Known: a second model call for a turn to both and for a relay; a second WebView for Paws and one for the box (`duo-render-spike.md` measured two pets, not the box); a thread that waits for the global shortcuts.'
3. **Do the two pets act on each other with a cause, or only run side by side?** The first sign of a cause was seen once with real brains (a pet's line taken up by the other). Whether the two feel alive together is the author's judgement, not a test.'
'
## What a good review looks like (the frame of section 6)'
Each point: the evidence in the repo (file, line), the change wanted, the benefit expected, the cost, the smallest test, and what would refute it. Separate what was checked, what is inferred and what is opinion. Do not take what a model wrote as true: open the file.'
'
## Questions the author has not answered and a reviewer may weigh in on'
- Should the scenes need both pets on the same floor, or should Paws climb to where Peak perches (`duo-prototype-notes.md`, "Seen and not solved")?'
- Paws on a free model (Groq) uses emoji and speaks informally: a stricter voice, or another model?'
- Is a profile file (L2) the right place for what a pet can do in a talk, or too much for two pets?'
- The global shortcuts register with Windows on a thread of their own: is that the right place, and is the privacy sentence in ADR-0142 enough?'
'
## Not to be reviewed'
The website, the stickers, the release routine and the macOS work; they belong to other sessions.'
