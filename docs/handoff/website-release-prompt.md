# Prompt for the website session, refilled at every release

**From:** the app session (Vinh and Claude)
**To:** whoever works on `site/` (GitHub Pages)
**Status:** a template (ADR-0033). At every release the app session fills in the version and what changed, and gives the author the text below to paste into the website session. This copy is filled for **0.3.1** (2026-11-10). The previous copies (0.2.5, 0.3.0) were acted on by the website session: the structure of its ADR-0034 is built and the site follows 0.3.0 (ADR-0039).

## The prompt (paste this)

You are the session that owns `site/` in the repository `vinhcodon/streakboom` (the website streakboom.com, served by GitHub Pages). The app session has just released **version 0.3.1** (Peak reacts like a cat in time, lines that vary, and a page that shows what Peak is told). Read these first, in this order: `CHANGELOG.md` (the 0.3.1 entry: the format the site reads), `README.md` (the reference text: features, privacy, status, roadmap), `spec/09-reactions.md` and `docs/decisions/0041-reactions-as-events-appraisal-beats-and-words.md` (the app session's new record; **list `docs/decisions/` and take the next free number before you write a record**), `docs/plans/verification-debt.md` (section C and the website rows), your own records ADR-0034 to ADR-0040, and `docs/decisions/0033-every-release-includes-the-same-housekeeping.md` (this routine). Do not edit anything outside `site/`, `scripts/build-site.mjs`, `scripts/site/` and `.github/workflows/pages.yml` without asking. Commit explicit paths only (ADR-0023). Say at the end what you did **not** verify.

### 1. Bring the site in line with the app (do this now)
- **Version and notes:** the site builds the version and the notes from `tauri.conf.json` and `CHANGELOG.md`; check that the 0.3.1 entry appears correctly in all three languages' pages and in `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, the structured data and the changelog page.
- **Privacy wording (important, a new sense):** the app now also asks the system **what kind of thing is just under Peak's feet** (the bare desktop, the taskbar, or some window) and keeps, only in memory and only while Peak stands there, an opaque number that means "the same window as a moment ago", so it can notice that the window it stands on was closed, minimized or dragged away. A window's class name is looked at only to tell the desktop and the taskbar from other windows and is dropped at once. It still never reads titles, contents, app names or keys, and nothing is written or sent. Add this to the "What Peak notices around it" item of `site/strings.mjs` and the privacy pages in the three languages (the README privacy bullet has the exact wording), and to any FAQ about what Peak reads. Do not describe it as Peak "seeing" the screen.
- **New behaviour to describe, as behaviour:** when the window it stands on goes away Peak freezes, jolts, cries out in a bigger speech bubble and falls to the bottom of the screen (nothing happens if another window is only brought in front of it); Peak no longer hops away by itself when you are not typing; repeating a poke, a scratch or a belly rub changes its answers in stages (playful, annoyed, resigned, forgiving); lines are said once each before any repeats and some use your name, the time or the weekday (only when it knows them); Settings, Brain has **What Peak is told**, a read-only page that shows the notes a brain is given about who Peak is, layer by layer, in the user's language, with the safety parts marked as locked.
- **Numbers and claims:** processor use about 4 to 6% of one core standing and about 9 to 11% median in normal use, on one machine (an A/B check of 0.3.1 against 0.3.0 found them equal); a hop costs about 0.34 seconds of one core; memory about 100 to 120 MB; **267 hand-written lines per language** (the home page and README say so). Do not promise macOS, code signing or a stats server. Groq, Claude and OpenAI are still **not tried with a real key**; Japanese is still a beta and the new Japanese and English lines are **not read by a native speaker**.
- **The interactive Peak and the hero tour:** the rig has a new startled face use (a freeze, a jolt, a cry); the site rebuilds it. Look at the tour once, and consider whether the tour should show the "window goes, Peak falls" moment (propose, do not just add).
- **The author's name** is **Vinh Codon** (check `site/` once more).
- **Releases:** GitHub keeps the newest two (`v0.3.1`, `v0.3.0`); never link a version-specific file; the download button points at `releases/latest`.

### 2. Propose the next improvements for search engines (SEO) and AI answer engines (GEO)
The structure of your ADR-0034 is built. As at every release, review it against the new facts and **propose** (as multiple choice for the author, with the trade-offs; the author decides and a record is written) what to improve next. Think about at least: coverage of the new facts for answer engines (the four brains, the three languages, what is free, what Peak does and does not read), the Japanese pages for Japanese search (titles, descriptions, `hreflang` including `ja`, questions people would ask in Japanese), whether a short comparison or "how it works" page would be quoted, structured data completeness and that it is generated from the repository so it cannot drift, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` wording, page speed on the hero canvas, and how we measure (Search Console and Bing Webmaster state, the fixed test questions for AI assistants, before and after). Say what you would **not** do and why. Do not build anything in this section until the author has chosen.

### 3. Report back
Summarise what changed, list what you did not verify (the state on the Pages host, other browsers, a phone, a Japanese reader), and add any unchecked item to the website section of `docs/plans/verification-debt.md`.

## How to refill this at the next release
Change the version and date in the status line and in the first paragraph; rebuild section 1 from the newest `CHANGELOG.md` entry and the README (what is new, the names, the numbers, the privacy wording, anything the site still says that is no longer true); keep section 2 (the SEO and GEO request is part of every release, ADR-0033) and replace its list by what is still open; keep section 3. Update the numbers from the latest measurement. Then hand the text to the author and note in `docs/handoff/README.md` that the prompt was sent.
