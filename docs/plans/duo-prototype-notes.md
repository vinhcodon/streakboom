# Duo prototype: what was built, what was checked, what was seen

2026-11-22. Branch `exp/duo-prototype` (worktree `streakboom-duo`), Preview only, behind the developer flag `STREAKBOOM_DUO=1` (off by default), never merged. Decisions: ADR-0139 (who appears), ADR-0140 (Paws chats too). Scenes and the rule: `duo-appearance-and-scenes.md`. The cost of two windows: `duo-render-spike.md`.

## Built (steps 1 to 3, job C of the handoff)
- **State and rule** (`duo.ts`, mirrored in `duo.rs` for the tray): who was out is restored at start; the tray click sends everyone in, or brings out the pet tucked last; the other comes out only when called; each pet comes out where it went in; the entrance is learned from the nearest edge of the work area (not behind the taskbar). The file is `duo.json`, not `pet.json` (Stable drops fields it does not know).
- **Paws window** (`/paws`, Mochi art of ADR-0131, side and front views): exists only while Paws is out; slides in and out; 100 px in a 140 px window like Peak; a small bob while it walks.
- **Scenes** (`scene.ts`): three wordless scenes (tidy the ball and the cushion; Peak shifts away and Paws does not chase; Paws pushes the ball and Peak stops it after a beat); one at a time, a token, the attention rule `mayAct`, a 5-minute gap, cancel on a touch or when a pet goes in. A shared ball or cushion is a third small window only while a scene runs. Peak's page holds its own wandering and shows the pose of the scene (one small block in `+page.svelte`, on the branch only).
- **No menu items.** The prototype is driven through `duo-cmd.txt` in its data folder (`call`, `scene[:id]`, `tray`, `cancel`, `dump`) and traced in `duo-log.txt`.

## Built (steps 4 and 5)
- **Chat commands** (`duo-command.ts`, `duo_command` in `duo.rs`): "gọi Paws ra đi", "Paws vào đi", "call Paws out", "Pawsを呼んで" and the like, understood on the device: no model, no key, no chat turn. Only a short message naming one pet and saying out or in.
- **Paws answers through the AI chat** (ADR-0140): its own voice layer (`persona/paws-voice.md`, from the character bible) with the locked layers shared with Peak; the pet named answers, with no name the one last spoken to, Paws only while it is out. Its key: its own entry if one was stored, else the shared key (Rust commands to store, ask and forget it; **no Settings control yet**). The bubble stands next to the pet that answers (same bubble window, placed against Paws for the length of the line).

- **The talk on screen (ADR-0141, steps 1 and 2 of its build order):** `conversation.ts` (room log, turn order, pass, aside, cancel; tested with a scripted model and a fake clock); every message that names Paws or both goes through it; one line at a time as a bubble beside the speaker with a name tag and colour; the listener turns its head first; a pass is a nod; the log with a name per speaker opens in the chat panel; the model is given a situation layer and its tags are checked by the code. Peak-only turns keep Peak's own chat path and are copied into the room.
- **Check without a key:** `STREAKBOOM_DUO_FAKE=1` gives canned replies and the line `type:<text>` in `duo-cmd.txt` sends a message the way the box does (ASCII only: the channel file is ASCII).

- **Pet profiles (ADR-0141 layer L2, step 5 of its build order):** `app/src/lib/pets/<pet>.profile.json` (speaks to the person, may pass, speaks to the other pet, longest line, one note sentence for the prompt); `pets.ts` reads it with bounds (a number is clamped, a wrong type is the default, an unknown key is ignored, the id is the pet's own); `Conversation` obeys it (a silent pet only gestures and costs no call, no pass, no aside, a word cap for each pet) and the prompt layer of the situation is built from it. Peak and Paws have the same values for now, so nothing changed on screen (T9 gives the same lines). Tested only by unit tests with a scripted model; no profile with other values has been shown to the author.

- **Relay (ADR-0141, situation 8):** "hỏi Paws xem...", "ask Peak...", "Pawsに聞いて" (short messages, local, decided by the code): the other pet turns to the one named (head turn, no words, no call), the one named answers the person (one call, role `relayed`, shown the question); one hop only; if the asker may not relay (profile flag `relays`) or the other pet is away it answers for itself; under Quiet the turn is shown and the answer is a gesture. Checked with canned replies (T10: `line peak turn to=paws`, then `line paws say to=person`, and the reverse) and by unit tests; not with a real brain.

- **The chat box, step 1 (ADR-0142):** a window `chatbox` (route `/chatbox`, 340x460 logical) at the bottom right of the work area; avatars (Peak: the mochi cat of the app icon, Paws: its front art), pet lines left with a name and colour at the start of a group, the person right, one avatar for a run of lines, a time at the start and after a gap of ten minutes, three dots while a pet is writing, recipient avatars Peak, Paws and Both with a dot for who is out, a pin, an input that takes Enter and Esc. The main page puts the person's message into the talk the moment it is sent and pushes the whole talk to the box; what is written in the box goes back as an action into the same `sendChat` path. A pet's answer is still a bubble above it (Peak-only answers too). Tray with the flag: one click shows or hides the box after 0.3 s, a double click sends everyone in or brings the last out; the box hides when it loses focus unless pinned, and a tray click right after a blur-hide does not show it again. **Checked by me** (canned replies, the developer channel for the tray, the real mouse and keyboard for the box): the box appears at x 1475, y 435 (1920x1030 work area, 125 percent); a message pasted into the real input appears on the right with a time; Peak answered on the left with its avatar and name, and in a bubble; to both, Peak then Paws, each on the left with its own avatar; a click on a pet hides the box; a single click shows it again; a single click with a double click straight after shows no box and sends both in; a double click again brings the last one out. **Not checked:** the real tray icon (the clicks were sent through the developer channel); the pin keeping the box open against a real loss of focus (the pin was clicked, and it did keep the box open while the person's own windows took the focus); a message to Paws alone shown in the box; the typing dots in a picture; the dark theme; the box on a second monitor; reduced motion.

## Checked by me, with the real program (script `scripts/windows/duo-test.ps1`, release build, identifier `com.streakboom.duospike`)
Tests: 427 TS tests, 6 Rust tests of `duo.rs`, `svelte-check` clean. On a 1920x1080 screen at 125 percent:
- Paws stands on the work area, on the same floor as Peak (the first build put it behind the taskbar: fixed).
- The three scenes run in order, one ball at a time, and the ball is gone at the end (the first build made several balls: fixed with a lock).
- A real mouse click on Peak during a scene ends it at once (about 6 s into a 14 s scene) and removes the ball.
- The tray sends both in and brings the last one out; a restart restores who was out.
- Chat commands through the developer channel (`cmd:paws:in` and so on): Paws in, a second send-in refused, out again, Peak in with Paws staying, Peak out refused.
- The bubble: a line said as Paws (`say:paws:...`, no model) stands centred above Paws; a line as Peak stands above Peak (window positions and a picture).
- The talk with canned replies (T9): to Paws alone one line; to both the lines come one after the other (the second after a beat), the first said in the room (`to=person+peak`), a pass shows as a pass, an aside as `to=peak`; the bubble stands centred over the speaker; the name tag shows; Paws turns toward the speaker.
- Live brains (2026-11-22, the author's Gemini key for Peak and Groq key for Paws, used by the program itself, `scripts/windows/duo-live.ps1`): both answered (Gemini about 1 s, Groq 1.2 to 2.5 s). Seen: the first Groq call failed (the default model is refused for the key; fixed with a fallback over the models the key is offered, it now uses `openai/gpt-oss-20b`); Paws answered like an assistant ("anything you need, just type") and reminded the person to wash their hands, too long: the Paws voice now says it is a companion and not an assistant, two short sentences; a message that opens with one name ("Peak, what do you think of Paws?") was taken for both pets: now for that pet. Peak's second line answered Paws' line ("Sleeping at this hour?"): the first sign of cause and effect between them. The text of the voices is for the author to judge.
- Real mouse and keyboard (`scripts/windows/duo-click.ps1`, canned replies): a double click on Paws opens the chat for it, the box standing beside Paws, with the chips Peak, Paws, Both; the chip Both moves the box beside Peak and the next message is answered by both (Peak, then Paws in its own bubble, Paws turning toward Peak); text typed with the real keyboard into the real chat box reaches the talk. A touch on Paws now ends a scene and the talk (its window takes the pointer only over the pet).
- **Seen and not solved, step 3:** the box is beside Peak for Both (a choice made in the code, not by the author); the chat box closes by itself when left alone (as before), so a script has to act fast; a Vietnamese typing mode changes typed letters (a test message came out as other letters); the box of a pet that wanders moves with it.
- **The whole chain with the real brains (2026-11-22, Gemini for Peak, Groq `openai/gpt-oss-20b` for Paws; real mouse, pasted text, `scripts/windows/duo-live2.ps1`):** a message to Paws is answered by Paws (2.3 s); to both, Paws then Peak, and Peak's line takes up what Paws said; a relay ("Peak, ask Paws what he did today") gives `peak turn`, then Paws answers what it did; the log of three parties shows names Paws, Peak, You in order with the chip Peak selected. Found and fixed: Peak, asked alone "what do you think of Paws?", answered as if it had never heard the name (its own chat did not know Paws was out); it now is told, and answered that Paws is a noisy dog that is not a bad companion. **Seen and not solved:** Peak's head turn cannot be seen when it stands still (the front art has no direction; Paws' turn can); Paws on Groq uses emoji, speaks informally and runs past two short sentences; Peak perched while Paws stands on the floor.
- Pictures read by me: sizes of Peak and Paws are close; Peak keeps its own walk and sleep poses in the scenes.

## Seen and not solved
- **The two pets must be on the same floor.** Peak often perches on a window edge (y 670 in the test, the floor is 867). A scene is refused then (logged). Open design question: Paws climbs to the same ground, or scenes run only on the floor.
- After a scene Peak's own wandering can walk into Paws and overlap it.
- Paws only bobs: the Mochi art has no legs to move; a walk cycle would be new art (needs the author).
- A touch on Paws does not cancel a scene (its window is click-through; the hit test of a second window is not built).
- The ball window cannot be narrower than 166 px (a Windows limit): it is invisible but it is wider than the ball.

## Not verified (each in `verification-debt.md`, V-181)
Two monitors, other scale factors, the attention mode of the real Peak (the prototype passes "free"), automatic start of a scene, reduced motion, the answers of Paws from a real key (nobody has seen one: whether the two voices are told apart, H-02), the second key as a person sees it (no Settings control), the commands typed into the real chat box, the Japanese voice of Paws, "both answer one after the other", talking to Paws when Peak is in (the chat box is Peak's), and above all the question of the handoff: do the two feel like they live together, or like two animations side by side. That is for the author to judge from the program.
