# Duo prototype: what was built, what was checked, what was seen

Updated 2026-11-22. Branch `exp/duo-prototype` (worktree `streakboom-duo`), **Preview only, behind the developer flag `STREAKBOOM_DUO=1` (off by default), never merged without the author.** Nothing in this note ships. Decisions: ADR-0139 (who appears), 0140 (Paws chats too), 0141 (the talk), 0142 (the chat box), 0143 (no naming of the pet), 0144 (what the pets call the person), 0145 (no buttons to choose who hears). The rule and the scenes: `duo-appearance-and-scenes.md`; the conversation design: `duo-conversation-design.md`; the cost of two windows: `duo-render-spike.md`; an entry for a reviewer: `../handoff/duo-review-request.md`. Every unverified item is in `verification-debt.md` (V-179 to V-187).

## How to run and check it
- **Data:** the prototype build uses its own identifier `com.streakboom.duospike` (a folder of its own in `%APPDATA%`), never the data of an installed Peak. Back up the real folder before running anything else on the machine.
- **Build:** from `app/`, `npm run tauri build -- --no-bundle --config src-tauri/tauri.duospike.conf.json`, then `git checkout src-tauri/Cargo.toml` (the Tauri CLI rewrites that file on Windows, V-178). Start the exe with `STREAKBOOM_DUO=1`. Other variable: `STREAKBOOM_DUO_FAKE=1` (canned replies, no key). **Changed 2026-11-23 (ADR-0150):** the developer flag `STREAKBOOM_PAWS_PROVIDER=groq` is gone with Groq; Paws speaks with the same brain as Peak (Gemini, Claude or OpenAI), with its own key if one was stored for it, else the shared key. The keys stay in the operating system's credential store and are used by the program; no script reads one.
- **Without menu items:** the developer channel is a line written to `duo-cmd.txt` in the data folder (`call`, `scene[:id]`, `tray`, `traysingle`, `traydouble`, `hotbox`, `hotall`, `pinbox`, `unpinbox`, `cmd:<pet>:<in|out>`, `say:<pet>:<text>`, `type:<text>` (ASCII only), `setkey:<box|all>:<combo>`, `opensettings`, `dump`); the answers are in `duo-dump.txt`, `duo-log.txt` and `duo-cmd-result.txt`.
- **Scripts** (`scripts/windows/`): `duo-test.ps1` (scenes, tray, commands, talk, relay with canned replies), `duo-live.ps1` and `duo-live2.ps1` (the real brains, `duo-live2` with the real mouse and pasted text), `duo-click.ps1`, `duo-box.ps1` (the chat box and the tray), `duo-keys.ps1` (the keys of the box), `duo-hotkeys.ps1` (the global shortcuts), `duo-name.ps1` (the name). **Rules the scripts keep:** a click is made only inside a window of the program under test, checked against the windows at that moment; keys are sent only while that program has the keyboard; the box is pinned by a developer command, not by a click; no script reads, prints or passes a key.

## Built
- **Two pets on one screen (ADR-0139).** `duo.ts` (state, pure) mirrored in `duo.rs`: who was out comes back at start; a double click on the tray sends everyone in, or with nobody out brings the pet tucked last; the other pet comes out only when the person asks the pet on the screen (in the chat or with the slash list); a pet comes out where it went in; the entrances are on the work area, not behind the taskbar. The state is `duo.json` (Stable drops unknown fields of `pet.json`). The Paws window (`/paws`, Mochi art of ADR-0131, side and front views, 100 px in a 140 px window like Peak) exists only while Paws is out; its hit test lets the pointer through except over the pet.
- **Scenes (`scene.ts`).** Three wordless scenes (tidy the ball and the cushion; Peak shifts away and Paws does not chase; Paws pushes the ball and Peak stops it), one at a time, a token, the attention rule, a 5-minute gap, cancelled by a touch on either pet. A shared ball or cushion is a third small window only while a scene runs.
- **Chat commands on the device** (`duo-command.ts`): "gọi Paws ra đi", "Paws vào đi", "call Paws out", "Pawsを呼んで" and the like; no model, no key. A relay ("hỏi Paws xem", "ask Peak ...", "Pawsに聞いて"): the other pet turns to the one named, which answers the person; one call, one hop.
- **Paws through the AI (ADR-0140).** `persona/paws-voice.md` (from the character bible: a companion, not an assistant, two short sentences) over the locked layers it shares with Peak; its key is its own entry if one was stored, else the shared one (Rust commands, no Settings control); another provider by the developer flag. Every call is traced (`brain peak|paws provider=... ok=... ms=...`), never a key.
- **The talk (ADR-0141).** `conversation.ts`: a room log, the turn order (the pet last spoken to first, the second sees the first and may pass), the aside, the relay, the cancel on a touch or when a pet goes in, a gate for the quota; tested with a scripted model and a fake clock. `pets.ts` and `pets/<pet>.profile.json` (layer L2: speaks to the person, may pass, speaks to the other pet, relays, longest line, one note for the prompt; bounded by the code). A situation layer tells each model who is present and who was spoken to. Lines are bubbles beside the speaker with a name tag, one at a time, the listener turns its head, a pass is a nod.
- **The chat box (ADR-0142).** A window `chatbox` (`/chatbox`, 340 by 460 logical) at the bottom right of the work area: avatars, the pets' lines left with a name tag, the person right, grouping, a time at the start and after ten minutes, three dots while a pet writes, two avatars with a dot for who is out (no buttons, ADR-0145), a pin, hide on losing focus, text in the chosen language. The tray: one click (after 0.3 s) shows or hides the box, a double click sends everyone in or brings the last one out; a click on the icon also hides the hidden-icons flyout of the taskbar. In the box: Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line, Esc clears and then hides, arrow up recalls, `/` opens the local commands (call out, send in, pin, clear, `/name`). Two global shortcuts (`duo_keys.rs`, `RegisterHotKey` on a thread of its own, Ctrl+Alt+P and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P by default, kept in `duo-keys.json`, a combination another program holds is refused) with a Shortcuts card in Settings, General page.
- **Who hears (ADR-0145).** A message goes to every pet that is out; a name ("Paws ơi") or `@Paws` picks one; a double click on Peak or Paws shows the box (if it was not), gives the keyboard to its field and, when both are out, writes `@Paws ` or `@Peak ` there (plain text); the old input above Peak is not opened any more in duo mode. With both pets in the field says so and a message that is not a local command is not sent; with nobody out a pet can be called out by name (the slash list, "call Paws out").
- **Names (ADR-0143, 0144).** The person no longer names the pet (the Settings row, the asking, the line to the model and the command words are gone; the field stays in `memory.json`). What the pets call the person is said in the chat ("call me Vinh", "gọi tôi là Vinh", "Vinhと呼んで", `/name Vinh`), one hint in the empty box, the field on the General page ("Hãy gọi tôi là"), both pets told the name; the old pop-up ask is skipped when the flag is on.
- **A change in code shared with the shipped program:** plain speech bubbles are shown without taking the keyboard (`show_without_focus` in `bubble_fit`). Found because a pet's line took the keyboard from the box the person was typing in. For the author to know before any merge.

## Checked by me, with the real program (release build, identifier `com.streakboom.duospike`, one 1920x1080 screen at 125 percent, Windows 10 build 19045)
- **Scenes and windows:** Paws stands on the work area on Peak's floor and is close to Peak in size; the three scenes run in order with one ball and clean up; a real click on either pet ends a scene at once; a restart restores who was out.
- **The tray, the commands, the box:** the tray clicks through the developer channel (single shows the box, a single and a double together show no box and send both in, a double again brings the last out); the commands in and out; the box at the bottom right; a message pasted into the real input appears on the right with a time and a pet answers on the left with its avatar and a bubble; to both, the second pet speaks after a beat; a click on a pet hides the box; a pin keeps it.
- **The keys of the box with real keys:** arrow up, Esc, Tab, Ctrl+1/3, Shift+Enter (two lines), the slash list (arrows, Enter; Send Paws in then Call Paws out; Clear this chat).
- **The global shortcuts with real keys:** both registered; a combination held by another program refused and the old one kept; no modifier, Shift alone and a duplicate refused; Ctrl+Alt+P shows and hides the box; Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P sends both in and brings the last out.
- **The real brains (2026-11-22; Gemini for Peak, Groq `openai/gpt-oss-20b` for Paws, the author's keys used by the program):** each answered (about 1 s and 1.2 to 2.5 s); to both, Paws then Peak and Peak's line takes up what Paws said; a relay gave `peak turn` then Paws answered; the log of three parties showed Paws, Peak and You in order. **Found and fixed on the way:** the default Groq model was refused for the key (a fallback over the models the key is offered); Paws answered like an assistant and reminded the person to wash their hands (the voice now says it is not an assistant); a message that opens with one name was taken for both pets; Peak alone, asked about Paws, answered as if it had never heard the name (its own chat did not know Paws was out).
- **Names and language:** "call me Linh" set the name; `/name Vinh` showed one item and set it; the hint shows in the empty box; the General page shows the field. After the author's remark the box and the Shortcuts card speak the chosen language: seen in Vietnamese ("Cả hai", "Nhắn cho Peak", "Hôm nay 15:38", "Hãy gọi tôi là Vinh", "Phím tắt").
- **The hidden-icons flyout (author's report, reproduced):** with the flyout open (Win+B, Enter) and the tray click sent through the developer channel, the box took the foreground and the flyout stayed visible; after the fix the flyout is hidden and the shell opens it again.
- **Who hears and the double click (ADR-0145), with the real mouse and keys, canned replies (`scripts/windows/duo-dblclick.ps1`):** a double click on Paws opened the box with the keyboard and `@Paws ` in the field and only Paws answered; a double click on Peak wrote `@Peak `; a message with no name was answered by both (Paws then Peak); with both in the avatars are grey without a dot, a message that is not a command stayed in the field and no line followed, and "call Paws out" brought Paws out.
- **Tests:** 491 TS tests, 135 Rust tests, `svelte-check` clean, `node scripts/audit-docs.mjs` clean.

## What the author found by trying it (2026-11-22)
The old input above Peak ("Nói gì đó đi…") still opened on a double click, a second box next to the chat box (it was the old bubble chat, reached by `onDblClick`; fixed by ADR-0145); the buttons choosing who hears were more than needed; and with only Paws out Peak could not be called out (the command took Peak for the pet that calls: now whichever pet is out calls the other, with nobody out either is brought out by name; checked with real keys, the slash list and a typed "gọi Peak ra đi", `scripts/windows/duo-callpeak.ps1`). Paws came out right but with the wrong side art (fixed to the Mochi side view); Paws too big and hidden behind the taskbar (fixed); a single input box on Peak with Paws answering in a plain bubble felt unnatural (led to ADR-0142); the hidden-icons flyout stayed open behind the box; the box was in English while the language was Vietnamese; the label of the name field; and that a renamed Peak is confused with "the" Peak (ADR-0143).

## The chat box as one overlay (ADR-0147, 2026-11-22)
The author asked for the box to be like the chat of a live stream; three builds followed, each tried by the author. What he found, in order: the pets' replies were gone from the box when the daily limit of 60 chats was reached (a silent gesture; now the pet says so); a first overlay with a "reading" and a "writing" layout and messages that expired was a surprise when the box shrank and the field vanished (one layout now, shown or hidden); a transparent background was hard to see (a dark scrim, dense at the bottom, tried on a white, a dark and a busy background); the avatars were too big and the right side of Paws's was cut (the image was scaled inside a rounded image; the circle now clips it, 22 px); an empty box showed a dark smear (the scrim shows only with a message); the pets answered in English to a Vietnamese message (the language is now the one the person writes in); and the box sat 16 px from the edge and the taskbar, a gap left from the floating card (flush now). The field says "Say something…" and nothing more. **2026-11-23:** the author asked that the box fade away by itself when the person has been elsewhere for a while; it begins after 20 s and takes 4 s, the pointer on it brings it back, and `/pin` keeps it. See `docs/decisions/0147-*` and V-188 to V-191.

## Mentions, the double click and the daily limit (ADR-0152, 2026-11-23)
The author asked for `@peak`, `@paws` and `@everyone` as in chat applications, found that a second double click on the other pet left `@Paws` in the field, and that `@Peak` could be offered again when it was already there, and that a command by message got no answer (the daily limit of 60 had been reached by a Peak-alone message, a silent return, and the phrase with a leading verb is not a command). See ADR-0152 and V-198; the polite-verb question is open.

## Seen and not solved
- **A script defect that hid a real test:** the guard that checks every click against the windows takes about a second; two clicks made through it were not a double click. Double clicks now check once and click twice at once (`DblClick`).
- **The two pets must be on the same floor for a scene.** Peak often perches on a window edge; the scene is refused then (logged). Open design question: Paws climbs, or scenes run only on the floor.
- After a scene Peak's own wandering can walk into Paws and overlap it.
- Paws only bobs (the Mochi art has no legs); a walk cycle would be new art. **2026-11-23, DU-11 step 1 (ADR-0154): with the two pictures it has Paws now stands looking at the person, turns its head to the pointer, to Peak and to a pet that spoke, reacts to a poke and a stroke, and can be carried and put down (V-200).** Peak's head turn cannot be seen when it stands still.
- Paws on Groq used emoji, spoke informally and often ran past two short sentences (the Groq run is history: **Groq was retired on 2026-11-23, ADR-0150**, and Paws's voice was rewritten, ADR-0149; Paws is heard again with the active brain).
- For *Both* the box stands beside Peak in the old bubble chat; in the new box this does not apply. The ball window cannot be narrower than 166 px (a Windows limit).
- A Vietnamese typing mode changes letters sent by a script; the keys of the box were not tried with a typing mode on.

## Dispatch and the daily count (T2d, ADR-0165 and ADR-0167, issue 22)
With the experiment there is one count (`ai.chat`) and one cap of 120 a day, for solo and visits alike; without it the cap stays 60. Every request is charged once, before it is issued, also if it fails or its reply is dropped, and saved at once: an invitation, the end of a visit or a restart on the same day never gives it back. Before a request the gate is asked for each pet: at 119 with two pets eligible, one asks and the other makes a gesture. Quiet, solo or in a visit, makes no request and no charge, only a gesture, and no cap line is spoken in Quiet. A send from the box carries an id (`mid`): a repeated delivery of it in one visit is dispatched once; two sends of the same text stay two. The canned replies of the developer channel (`STREAKBOOM_DUO_FAKE=1`) now pass the same gate and charge, so a test build can run out of the day's 120 like any other; `[fake:rate_limit]` or `[fake:network]` in a message makes a canned failure. The rollover is the local calendar date (`dayKey`); the cooldown after a rate limit is kept across midnight.

## Entering a visit (T2a, ADR-0167, issue 23)
With Paws as a chat member, an invitation (`@Paws` at the start, or the typed command) is an entry with its own generation. Nothing is shown or saved while a word is being composed in the box; then the membership save and the box are started together, and the visit commits when both have answered, in either order, the entry is still current and no composition is active. Only then are the held words sent, once. If the membership is refused, the box cannot be shown, or 5000 ms pass without both answers (the entry deadline, owned by the pet page), the entry fails: a saved membership is undone, and so is one saved later; the invitation and the messages waiting behind it go back to the field, in order, once; Peak says the existing line for a call that changed nothing (not in Quiet); no request is made. Closing the box during the entry ends it at that instant, with the same return and no line. In Quiet the entry commits silently and the waiting messages go back. A double click, the box shortcut or a second start during an entry waits for it and then focuses its box; one made before a close is dropped. Returned text carries an id: the box applies it once and acknowledges it, the page sends it again when the box is ready if it was not acknowledged, and the whole text goes back even beyond the 300 characters of typing. The developer channel can refuse (`[fake:join-fail]`) or slow (`[fake:join-slow]`, 3 s) the membership save, and `boxclose` closes the box as its Esc does.
