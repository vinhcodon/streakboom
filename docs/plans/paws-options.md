# Paws: three cuter options (second look)

**Status:** drawn 2026-11-15 by the sticker session. The author saw the first reference sheet (`paws-reference-sheet.md`) and said Paws is not as cute as Peak. **Only three options of the reference are drawn, no sticker.** Nothing is added to the app.

## What changed against the first sheet
The Peak recipe, applied harder: a **bigger head** and a smaller body, a **small muzzle low on the face**, **bigger wide-set eyes** with two highlights, blush, soft round shapes. The palette, the light rim line, the white toed paws and the tan brow spots are kept (so it still holds on a dark ground). No arms.

## The options (all in [`assets/paws/`](assets/paws/))
| | Shape | Ears | Mouth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Mochi** | head and body are one round bean (the closest to Peak) | soft floppy ears | small closed smile |
| **B. Big head** | a big round head on a small body, bushy tail up | upright ears, the tips folded (the most "collie") | open happy mouth, tongue |
| **C. Sitting** | sits like a loaf, fluffy tail curled up | long drooping ears | big white face mask, closed smile |

Files: [`paws-options.png`](assets/paws/paws-options.png) (the three side by side, with small versions on a dark ground), [`paws-options-check.png`](assets/paws/paws-options-check.png) (256, 64 and 32 px on #ffffff, #1e1f22 and #2b2d31), `paws-option-a/b/c.svg`, built by `build_paws_options.py`.
