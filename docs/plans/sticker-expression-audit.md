## 2. Findings
1. **The set is subdued.** Eyes: 7 of 16 are closed arcs (01, 02, 04, 08, 10, 11, 14), 5 are half-lidded deadpan (03, 05, 06, 15, 16), the rest open. Mouths: 6 are a flat line (03, 05, 06, 07, 13, 15). Nothing has a wide-open mouth for laughter or crying. At 120 px the face is what is read, so many stickers share one face (closed arcs and a small smile).
2. **Near-duplicates.** 02 "Sướng quá~" and 11 "Cảm ơn nha!" have the same face (closed arcs, smile) and differ only by the hearts. 14 "Hì hì." also has closed arcs; it is saved by the paw over the mouth. 06 and 10 are both "sad ears" (overwhelmed, sorry).
3. **Gaps in the emotions people send** (compared with the uses listed in `sticker-set-v1-direction.md` section 1): **laughing out loud** ("haha"), **crying** ("huhu"), **celebrating** (the clap went; nothing high-energy and happy is left), **agreeing** ("ok") and **saying no** ("không"), **embarrassed** (shy), **mildly angry** (05 is a sulk, not anger). Love is covered by 11.
4. **Rain (09)** is a weather state, not an emotion; it will be sent rarely compared with the gaps above.
5. **Sarcasm share:** 03, 05, 14, 15, 16 tease: 5 of 16, about 31% (ADR-0050 aimed at 29%).

## 3. Proposals (for the author to choose; nothing here is drawn)
**Make the near-duplicates differ**
- **11 "Cảm ơn nha!"** with the front paws pressed together under the chin and a small bow, hearts kept: the thanks gesture, distinct from 02 by its body.
- **10 "Xin lỗi nhé."** with big watery eyes (open, extra highlights), ears down and a sweat drop (the rig has the drop on the hanging pose); eyes open, not closed, so it differs from 11 and 02.
- **02 "Sướng quá~"** unchanged (bliss with the closed arcs is the app's own `purr`).

**Add the missing emotions (up to 4 more; the limit is 20)** — each with a face that is not a closed arc and a small smile:
| Idea | Text (proposal) | Face and gesture |
|---|---|---|
| Laughing out loud | Haha! | closed arcs tilted, a wide open mouth, tears of joy, head back |
| Crying | Huhu… | wide wet eyes, two streams of tears, ears down, mouth wobbling |
| Celebrating (replaces the clap) | Hoan hô! | both paws up, mouth open, eyes arcs, confetti |
| Agreeing | Ok nè! | one paw making a circle, half-closed eyes, small smile |
| Saying no | Không nha. | two paws crossed in front, eyes open and firm, flat mouth |
| Shy | Ngại quá… | strong blush, eyes aside, a paw covering the face |
| Mildly angry | Giận rồi! | puffed cheeks, eyes narrowed, small puffs (no violence: Zalo bans it) |

**Swap rain** for one of the above if the author prefers an emotion to the weather.

## 4. Constraints to keep
At most 4 words of text, nothing that Zalo bans, flat style, no app name (ADR-0049); no guilt (charter). Every gesture above (circle, crossed paws, tears) is sticker art, not an app pose; the face comes from the rig's dials (lid, mouth, cheek, ears).

**Update (2026-11-11, later): "Hoan hô!" and "Không nha." were removed by the author, so the set is 18 and a celebration and a firm "no" are gaps again.** Ideas to try if the author wants them back, each softer: a celebration with the paws up and a bounce (small hops drawn as two motion lines) with the same smiling face; a "no" with one paw raised flat like a stop sign and a gentle head shake instead of crossed arms and brows.
