## 3. What this changes
1. **The website.** The page shows one pack. The list is 33, so the manifest and the page must show 33 (the page should not assume 20). **Hoan hô! and Làm chung nhé? leave the page when it moves to the new list.** Until Vinh approves the list and a handoff is written, the website stays as it is (the answer given on 2026-11-12: the page moves once, when ready).
2. **Zalo (not planned now).** A set has 12 to 20 stickers, so 33 would be two sets, for example 17 and 16 by type; not decided and not needed for the website.
3. **Languages.** The 18 kept stickers have Vietnamese, English and Japanese (`set-v1`), but **the text of three of them changes** ("Rất tiếc." replaces "Quá sức mèo rồi.", "Yêu quá!" and "Sinh nhật vui vẻ!" are new wording), and the **15 new ones have Vietnamese only**. Vinh said earlier that the new office ones are Vietnamese first; the cute ones could have English and Japanese. Which languages the new ones get is a question (question 2).
4. **File numbers.** The files of the final list are numbered 01 to 33 in the order above (so a type is easy to find and the page can group by type from the manifest). The numbers in `set-v1` change.
