## 3. Review: quotes or pictures that are close to each other
Done on the 32 above (before "Rất tiếc." was dropped; the numbers in the tables of this section are those of the 32-list). **Vinh answered on 2026-11-12 and the changes are applied (see the Edit column): open eyes for Chúc mừng, Sinh nhật and Cảm ơn; a big yawn for Buồn ngủ; sparkles instead of hearts for Cảm ơn; "Hứ!" turned away for Hừm; Ơ! made a real shock and kept next to Ủa?.** The tables below are the review as it was proposed. "Same meaning" means a person might hesitate between the two; "same picture" means they look alike at 120 px.

### 3.1 Close in meaning
| Pair | What they share | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| **Ơ! / Ủa?** (21, 22) | both are a "huh?" reaction (surprise and puzzlement) | **Most alike.** Keep both and sharpen: Ơ! = a shock (the mouth open wide, the pupils tiny, the body leaning back); Ủa? stays puzzled with the question marks. Or drop "Ơ!" |
| **Hừm. / Gớm!** (27, 25) | both are a grumble of mild disapproval | Keep "Gớm!" (it has its own props) and turn "Hừm." into a sulk with the head turned away ("Hứ!"), or drop "Hừm." |
| **Xin lỗi nhé. / Rất tiếc.** (07, 08) | both say "I regret it" | Keep: one apologises, the other shrugs ("nothing to be done"). The shrug picture makes them look different |
| **Chúc mừng nha! / Sinh nhật vui vẻ!** (09, 10) | both congratulate | Keep: one is a general congrats, the other an occasion. They carry different props (a flower, a cake) |
| **Ok nè! / Giỏi quá.** (31, 29) | both approve | Keep: "agree" against "well done"; one is a wink and a ring, the other a thumb up |
| **Haha! / Hì hì. / Sướng quá~** (12, 14, 13) | laughing, giggling and bliss | Keep: three different intensities (loud, covered, quiet) |
| **Buồn ngủ… / Ngủ ngon.** (03, 02) | both are about sleep with a "z" | Keep; but see 3.2 |
| **Không nha. / Còn lâu!** (32, 24) | both refuse | Keep: a polite "no" against a mocking one |
