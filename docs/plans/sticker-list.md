## 3. Review: quotes or pictures that are close to each other
Done on the 32 above (before "Rất tiếc." was dropped; the numbers in the tables of this section are those of the 32-list). **Vinh answered on 2026-11-12 and the changes are applied (see the Edit column): open eyes for Chúc mừng, Sinh nhật and Cảm ơn; a big yawn for Buồn ngủ; sparkles instead of hearts for Cảm ơn; "Hứ!" turned away for Hừm; Ơ! made a real shock and kept next to Ủa?.** The tables below are the review as it was proposed. "Same meaning" means a person might hesitate between the two; "same picture" means they look alike at 120 px.
