## 1. The three categories
| Category | What it is for | Voice | Faces and bodies it uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dễ thương** (cute) | Sweet, warm, "aww": greetings, love, thanks, sorry, cheering up, celebrations, a cat being small and soft | Gentle, a little shy; the cheek is in the cuteness, not in the words | closed arcs, big shiny eyes, hearts, blush |
| **Bạn bè** (friends, cheeky) | Banter between friends: teasing, "yeah right", gossip, shrugs, dares, plans to go out | Deadpan, quick, a little vain; teases the situation, never the person's worth | half lids, tilts, one paw gestures, side looks |
| **Công sở** (office, cheeky) | Work chat: meetings, deadlines, "seen", Monday and Friday, mistakes, excuses | Dry and knowing; light about the boss | props (laptop, mug, clock), panic and resignation faces |

A sticker is used in more than one place (a "thanks" goes to a boss as well as a friend), so on the web each sticker has **one home category and may carry tags** (the manifest gets a `category` field and an optional `tags` list); the page shows the three packs as tabs.

## 5. What it changes
1. **The website.** The current page shows one pack of 20 from `set-v1` in three languages. Three packs means a pack selector (three tabs) and a manifest with a `category`. **The website session would change the page; this is a new handoff, written after approval.** Until then the current set stays as it is: nothing on the site changes by this document.
2. **File numbers.** Inside each pack the files are numbered 01 to 20 again (Zalo wants 01, 02, ... in a set), so a current sticker's file number changes when it moves into its pack. The `set-v1` folder stays until the site has moved over, as `set-v0` did.
3. **Languages.** Current stickers keep vi, en, ja. The 10 new Cute stickers can have three languages too (their texts translate: "Hug!", "Miss you!", "Meow~"). The 11 Friends can have Vietnamese and English at best (the slang does not carry), and the 19 Office are Vietnamese only (Vinh's choice on 2026-11-12), so **the three packs do not have the same languages**; the page shows a pack in a language only if it exists, and says so.
4. **A pack is a valid store set** (12 to 20 stickers, 360 x 360): each pack could be submitted to Zalo later as its own set, which was the original plan (ADR-0049). This is not decided here.
5. **Effort.** 40 new drawings (10 + 11 + 19), each with one new prop or gesture, plus rounds of notes as in pack 1, which took many. The three packs are not equally easy: Cute is the easiest (rig faces, hearts, one prop); Friends needs gestures; Office needs the most props.
